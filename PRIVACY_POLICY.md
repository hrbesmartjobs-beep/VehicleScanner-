# Privacy Policy – Vehicle Scanner

**Developer:** VIGHNESH
**Effective date:** 25 November 2026

Vehicle Scanner reads vehicle number plates in photos and checks them against a list of
registration numbers that you keep on your own phone. Everything happens on the device.

## Data the app handles
- **Photos** that you take with the camera or pick from your gallery. They are processed on the phone. A copy and the plate crops are kept in the app's private storage so you can see your scan history.
- **Vehicle registration numbers** that you type in or import from Excel/CSV files, plus the scan results.
- **App settings** such as theme and accuracy thresholds.

## What we do NOT do
- The app does **not** have internet permission. It cannot send any data off your phone.
- We do not collect, upload, sell or share any personal data, photos or registration numbers.
- The app contains no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party data collection.
- No account is needed.

## Permissions
The app requests no runtime permissions. Photos come from the Android photo picker or the system camera app, and files are opened through the Android file picker. You choose each file yourself.

## Storage, backup and deletion
All data is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. You can create a backup file yourself, and you choose where it is saved. You can delete all data at any time with **Settings → Delete all local data**, or by uninstalling the app.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.
